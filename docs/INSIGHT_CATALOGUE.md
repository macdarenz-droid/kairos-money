# Insight catalogue — version 1

Every rule ranks the conditional retained amount × ease (2; triage 3), shows at most three, and suppresses a kind after two dismissals. A triage state allows at most one. The action is an if-then with an observed purchase/renewal/pay trigger. All effects are conditional arithmetic scenarios; no experimental effect size is copied into dollars.

| Kind | Signal and threshold | Evidence | Conditional projection | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| small_leaks | small_leak_index: ≥10 purchases, ≥10% of discretionary spend | selected sub-15-currency-unit settled discretionary rows | selected total × 1/5 | pain-of-paying; implementation-intentions |
| recurring_review | subscription_drag ≥20% of recorded net income | recorded/detected recurring rows | recurring total × 1/10 | mental-accounting; implementation-intentions |
| payday_plan | payday_decay ≥0.5 across ≥2 complete cycles | complete-cycle spending rows | days 1–3 total × 1/10 | present-bias; implementation-intentions |
| buffer_first | distress plus observed small spending | selected small-purchase rows | selected total × 1/10 | scarcity; implementation-intentions |

The chosen fractions are disclosed examples for a scenario, not predictions of compliance. Users can inspect the evidence and decline. Periods are the actual covered trailing window, not falsely annualised/monthly savings. Triage does not recommend spending or offer optimisation/gamification. If no eligible scenario exists, the static essentials/support panel remains.

The small-leak definition uses 15 units of the selected currency; currencies are never silently converted. Unknown categorisation is not labelled discretionary. Research catalogue and ADR 0008 explain limitations. Optional reflections are not the Klontz instrument and are labelled non-psychological; that optional scale remains disabled.
