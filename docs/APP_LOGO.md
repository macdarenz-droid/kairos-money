# App logo

The owner chose option C, "the right moment", on 2026-09-24: a ring with a gap and a dot, with savings bars rising inside. Kairos means the right moment.

- Web: `public/branding/kairos-logo.svg`.
- Android: `drawable/kairos_launcher.xml` (adaptive icon foreground on `kairos_icon_background`) and `drawable/kairos_mark.xml` (white notification icon).
- Kairos AI mark: `public/branding/kairos-ai.svg`. It uses the same ring with a sparkle inside, and animates only while Kairos AI is thinking.
