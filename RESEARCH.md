Session 3 is authorized. The original brief's exact four axes, six archetypes, signal formulas and named-effect list have not been recovered. This document does not substitute new definitions for them. No intelligence feature is shipped by this checkpoint.

## Evidence boundaries

- Mani, Mullainathan, Shafir and Zhao (2013), Poverty Impedes Cognitive Function, Science, DOI 10.1126/science.1238041. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1238041 . Experiments and a field comparison examine cognitive performance under financial concerns. This supports considering cognitive load in interface design; it does not validate diagnosing a user from spending records or an app-specific distress threshold. Read alongside the published comment and response (DOIs 10.1126/science.1246680 and 10.1126/science.1246799).
- Gollwitzer and Sheeran (2006), Implementation intentions and goal achievement: a meta-analysis of effects and processes. Author publication indexed at https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/ . Supports studying if-then action plans. It does not establish a dollar saving from Kairos or guarantee an individual's outcome. Full-text appraisal and exact record link remain part of the unfinished catalogue.
- Financial Counselling Australia, https://www.financialcounsellingaustralia.org.au/ , checked 13 September 2026: National Debt Helpline 1800 007 007 and ndh.org.au provide access to free financial counselling. This is a support resource, not evidence for a behavioural score.
