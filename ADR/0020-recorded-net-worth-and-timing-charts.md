# Recorded net worth and timing charts

Manual valuations are separate from ledger cashflows. An asset or liability has stable item identity and dated positive values; a liability subtracts from the total. Updating or removing a valuation never creates transactions or coverage. Encrypted app_settings retains the records under a namespaced key so existing export, backup, restore and deletion include them without altering the frozen 16-table export contract.

The chart explicitly describes recorded holdings, not complete or live net worth. Imported account balances are not silently combined with manual valuations, avoiding double counting. Currencies are separate. Earlier recorded item values are carried forward with their original dates visible. Users must include each holding once and update estimates.

Payday charts require at least three recorded pay dates. Each covered day belongs to its latest pay date; pending entries, transfers and uncovered days never enter the spending numerator or denominator. Unknown offsets break the line. Recurring timelines show actual source payments only. Neither chart makes a psychological diagnosis or fabricates missing events.

Alternatives rejected: treating valuations as income, mixing currencies without stored exchange rates, carrying unexplained balances into safe-to-spend, or inferring recurring payments in missing coverage. Real Android and both-theme review remain release requirements.
